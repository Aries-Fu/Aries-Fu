## 👩‍💻 About Me

I am a PhD candidate in **Model-Driven Engineering at Adelaide University**, with my doctoral thesis submitted and currently under examination.

My research focuses on improving interoperability across **heterogeneous systems** through advanced modelling methods. In particular, I investigate approaches that integrate **multi-level modelling** and **multi-view modelling** to support both **vertical** and **horizontal interoperability**.

A central theme of my work is **consistency management** in collaborative modelling environments. I am particularly interested in projection-based multi-view modelling, where multiple stakeholder-specific views are derived from a shared underlying model. This architecture enables consistency between views to be managed systematically through their relationships with the underlying model.

## 📌 Selected Research Projects

### Combined Modelling for Interoperability

Developing integrated modelling approaches that combine multi-level and multi-view modelling to improve interoperability across heterogeneous systems.

### Consistency Management Across Views

Investigating how consistency can be systematically maintained when multiple stakeholder-specific views overlap on a shared underlying model.

### Digital Twin Modelling

Exploring modelling methods for developing interoperable and consistent digital twin systems in complex and collaborative environments.
